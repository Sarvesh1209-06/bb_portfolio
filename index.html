ABOUT BIG BUDDY FITNESS

MORE THAN A GYM.
YOUR FITNESS COMMUNITY.

Located on the 1st Floor of Thirumagal Plazza on Tiruppur Road (above Trends) in Kangeyam, BIG BUDDY FITNESS STUDIO is dedicated to helping individuals build real, functional strength and sustainable discipline.

Whether your journey is just beginning or you are taking competitive bodybuilding to the next level, our studio provides a high-energy training ground equipped with Matrix Fitness machinery, dedicated Olympic racks, a turf sprint track, and hands-on professional coaching.
